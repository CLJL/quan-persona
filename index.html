Turn survey responses into data-driven personas.

quan-persona is a behavioural segmentation and persona analytics platform. It uses unsupervised machine learning to group survey respondents by how they actually behave, then turns each group into a persona and a customer journey map.

Use it to understand customer behaviour, employee experience, or where a service can improve, with evidence from your own data instead of assumptions.

Why quan-persona

Evidence-based segmentsGroups come from real survey responses, not predefined profiles.
Your own measurementsConfigure the questionnaire, measures and analytical dimensions to fit your research question.
Less manual analysisClustering and dashboards are generated automatically for compatible datasets.
Interactive insightExplore each segment's characteristics and distributions visually.
Local by designYour survey data is processed in your browser (see Data privacy).

How it works

1
Design your questionnaireIn the Schema designer, define your research questions, behavioural dimensions and measurement criteria. Download the questionnaire and send it to respondents by email or through your survey platform.
2
Upload and map your dataImport survey responses as a CSV. The portal matches columns to your schema, and you can correct the matching before analysis.
3
Discover behavioural segmentsDBSCAN first sets aside respondents who fit no group (about 10%), then K-means groups the rest. You choose K from 3 to 8, and a K check reports how well each choice is supported. The same file always gives the same clusters.
4
Explore your insightsReview the results in three views: Persona Dashboard, Journey Map, and Analytics & DB.

What you get

Persona DashboardOne page per segment: demographics, working day, tools, pain points, keywords and quotes. Edit it and download the persona as an image.
Journey MapStage-by-stage satisfaction, actions, tools and pain points for a segment, with an optimisation opportunity for each stage.
Analytics & DBCluster discovery with radar and heat-map views, plus distributions and response charts for all respondents.

Who it's for

Product OwnersUnderstand agent and customer journeys and find operational pain points.
UX ResearchersBuild evidence-based user segments and complement qualitative work with quantitative evidence.
Service DesignersSee where experiences differ and where a service can improve.
CX ConsultantsSegment customers by behaviour and spot experience gaps.

Data privacy

quan-persona is built to work local-first. Survey data is processed on your device, in your browser, and is not uploaded to a cloud server for analysis. Custom schemas you create are saved in your browser's local storage on that device.

This keeps control of your data with you and reduces unnecessary exposure of sensitive survey information.

Quick answers about data, privacy and how quan-persona works.

What is quan-persona?
quan-persona is a behavioural segmentation and persona analytics platform. You bring survey responses as a CSV; it groups respondents by how they actually behave, then turns each group into a persona and a customer journey map.
Does my data leave my computer?
No. Survey data is processed on your device, in your browser, and is not uploaded to a cloud server for analysis. Your browser does load the page itself from wherever it is hosted, but your responses are not sent anywhere.
Is my data saved after I close the page?
Your uploaded dataset is saved in your browser, on this device only, and is not sent to any server. When you come back or refresh the page, the portal loads it again. It stays until you upload another CSV, which replaces it, or you use Restore Default Data in the Data menu, which deletes the saved dataset from your browser for good. Because the data stays in your browser, please do not upload your CSV files on a shared or public computer. If you have to, use Restore Default Data before you leave. Custom schemas, your language choice and a few display settings are also kept in your browser's local storage. Edits to personas are not saved, so download the persona as an image before you leave.
What is the sample data I see when I open the portal?
A synthetic insurance-agent dataset that demonstrates how the portal works. Upload your own CSV to replace it, and use Restore Default Data in the Data menu to bring it back.
What CSV format do I need?
One row per respondent and one header row, with multi-select answers separated by a semicolon. Column names don't have to match exactly: the portal matches them to your schema, and you can correct the matching before analysis. Open the CSV Column Guide in the Data menu for the exact columns, and download the CSV template if you want a ready-made header.
How many respondents do I need?
With fewer than 15 respondents the portal puts everyone in a single group, because that is too few for stable clustering. The K check needs at least 24 respondents. In general, more respondents give more stable groups. The K check runs automatically for up to 3,000 respondents.
How does it decide how many groups there are?
DBSCAN first sets aside respondents who fit no group (about 10%), then K-means groups the rest. You choose K from 3 to 8 with the slider, and the K check reports how well each choice is supported using a silhouette score and stability.
Will I get the same result every time?
Yes. The same file always gives the same clusters.
Can I use my own questionnaire and measures?
Yes. In the Schema designer, define your research questions, behavioural dimensions and measurement criteria. Then download the questionnaire from the Data menu and send it to respondents by email or through your survey platform. quan-persona does not send or collect questionnaires itself: you bring the responses back as a CSV.
Can I edit and export the personas?
Yes. You can edit a persona's title, description, photo and quote, and download the persona as an image.
Which browsers does it work in?
Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox.
Is quan-persona open source?
Yes. It is released under the MIT License.

關於資料、隱私，以及 quan-persona 如何運作的快速解答。

什麼是 quan-persona？
quan-persona 是一個行為分群與人物誌分析平台。你提供 CSV 格式的問卷資料，它會依受訪者實際的行為模式分群，再為每一群產出人物誌與顧客旅程地圖。
我的資料會離開我的電腦嗎？
不會。問卷資料在你的裝置上、於瀏覽器內處理，不會上傳到雲端伺服器進行分析。瀏覽器仍會從網站所在位置載入頁面本身，但你的填答資料不會被傳送到任何地方。
關閉頁面後，我的資料會被保存嗎？
您上傳的資料集會儲存在這台裝置的瀏覽器中，不會傳送到任何伺服器。下次回來或重新整理頁面時，平台會自動載入。資料會一直保留，直到您上傳另一份 CSV 加以取代，或使用「資料」選單中的「恢復預設數據」；使用後，已儲存的資料集會從瀏覽器中永久刪除。由於資料會保留在瀏覽器中，請勿在共用或公用電腦上上傳您的 CSV 檔案；若不得不使用，離開前請先執行「恢復預設數據」。自訂的衡量架構、語言選擇與部分顯示設定，也會儲存在瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。人物誌的編輯內容不會保存，離開前請先把人物誌下載成圖片。
一開啟平台就看到的資料是什麼？
那是一組模擬的保險業務員資料，用來示範平台怎麼運作。上傳你自己的 CSV 就會取代它，也可以在「資料」選單中使用「恢復預設資料」把它找回來。
CSV 需要符合什麼格式？
每位受訪者一列，另有一列標題列，複選題的答案以分號隔開。欄位名稱不必完全一致：平台會自動比對你的衡量架構，分析前也可以自行調整對應。確切的欄位請開啟「資料」選單中的「CSV 欄位說明」，若想要現成的標題列，也可以下載 CSV 範本。
需要多少位受訪者？
受訪者少於 15 位時，平台會把所有人歸成同一群，因為樣本太少，無法穩定分群。K 值檢查至少需要 24 位受訪者。一般來說，受訪者越多，分群越穩定。受訪者在 3,000 位以內時，K 值檢查會自動執行。
平台怎麼決定要分成幾群？
先以 DBSCAN 排除不屬於任何一群的受訪者（約 10%），再以 K-means 將其餘受訪者分群。你可以用滑桿在 3 到 8 之間選擇 K 值，K 值檢查則會以輪廓係數與穩定度，說明每個選擇的支持程度。
每次分析的結果都一樣嗎？
一樣。同一份檔案，每次都會得到相同的分群結果。
可以用我自己的問卷與衡量指標嗎？
可以。在 Schema 設計工具中，定義你的研究問題、行為構面與衡量標準。接著從「資料」選單下載問卷，再透過電子郵件或問卷平台發送給受訪者。quan-persona 本身不負責發送或回收問卷，你需要把回收的答案整理成 CSV 帶回來。
可以編輯並匯出人物誌嗎？
可以。你可以編輯人物誌的標題、描述、照片與代表性語錄，並把人物誌下載成圖片。
支援哪些瀏覽器？
任何新版瀏覽器都可以：Chrome、Edge、Safari 與 Firefox。
quan-persona 是開源的嗎？
是的，採用 MIT 授權。
