Beta

Turn survey responses into data-driven personas.

quan-persona is a behavioural segmentation and persona analytics platform. It uses unsupervised machine learning to group survey respondents by how they actually behave, then turns each group into a persona and a customer journey map.

Use it to understand customer behaviour, employee experience, or where a service can improve, with evidence from your own data instead of assumptions.

Why quan-persona

Evidence-based segmentsGroups come from real survey responses, not predefined profiles.
Your own measurementsConfigure the questionnaire, measures and analytical dimensions to fit your research question.
Less manual analysisClustering and dashboards are generated automatically for compatible datasets.
Interactive insightExplore each segment's characteristics and distributions visually.
Local by designYour survey data is processed in your browser (see Data privacy below, or check the FAQ).

How it works

1
Design your questionnaireIn the Schema designer, define your research questions, behavioural dimensions and measurement criteria. Download the questionnaire to send to respondents by email or through your survey platform.
2
Upload and map your dataImport survey responses as a CSV. The portal matches columns to your schema, and you can correct the matching before analysis.
3
Discover behavioural segmentsDBSCAN first sets aside respondents who do not belong to sufficiently dense clusters as potential outliers or noise (the proportion varies based on data distribution and clustering parameters), then K-means groups the rest. You choose K from 3 to 8, and a K check reports how well each choice is supported. The same file always gives the same clusters.
4
Explore your insightsReview the results in three views: Persona Dashboard, Journey Map, and Analytics & DB.

What you get

Persona DashboardOne page per segment: demographics, working day, tools, pain points, keywords and quotes. Edit it.
Journey MapStage-by-stage satisfaction, actions, tools and pain points for a segment, with an optimisation opportunity for each stage.
Analytics & DBCluster discovery with radar and heat-map views, plus distributions and response charts for all respondents.

Who it's for

Product OwnersUnderstand user and customer journeys, identify behavioural patterns, and uncover pain points along the customer journey.
UX ResearchersBuild evidence-based user segments and complement qualitative work with quantitative evidence.
Service DesignersSee where experiences differ and where a service can improve.
CX ConsultantsSegment customers by behaviour and spot experience gaps.

Data privacy

quan-persona is built to work local-first. Survey data is processed on your device, in your browser, and is not uploaded to a cloud server for analysis. Custom schemas you create are saved in your browser's local storage on that device.

This keeps control of your data with you and reduces unnecessary exposure of sensitive survey information.

Your uploaded dataset also stays in your browser on this device. Please do not upload your CSV files on a shared or public computer. If you have to, use Restore Default Data in the Data menu before you leave.

Beta

把問卷資料，變成有數據依據的人物誌。

quan-persona 是一個行為分群與人物誌分析平台。它運用非監督式機器學習，依受訪者實際的行為模式分群，再為每一群產出人物誌與顧客旅程地圖。

無論你想了解顧客行為、員工體驗，或是找出服務可以改善的地方，都能用自己手上的資料說話，而不是憑感覺猜測。

為什麼選擇 quan-persona

以證據為基礎的分群分群來自真實的問卷填答，不是事先設定好的樣板。
自訂你的衡量架構依研究問題，自行設定問卷題目、衡量指標與分析構面。
減少人工分析相容的資料集，系統會自動完成分群並產出儀表板。
互動式洞察用視覺化圖表，探索各群的特徵與分佈。
資料留在本機問卷資料在你的瀏覽器中處理（詳見下方資料隱私說明，或查看常見問題）。

運作方式

1
設計問卷在 Schema 設計工具中，定義研究問題、行為構面與衡量標準。再下載問卷，透過電子郵件或問卷平台發送給受訪者。
2
上傳並對應資料匯入 CSV 格式的問卷資料，系統會依你的衡量架構自動比對欄位，分析前也可以自行調整對應。
3
找出行為分群先以 DBSCAN 依資料密度與分佈識別並排除離群值與雜訊點（比例依數據特徵與分群參數而異），再以 K-means 將其餘受訪者分群。K 值可在 3 到 8 之間自行選擇，並提供 K 值檢查，了解每個選擇的支持程度。同一份檔案，每次都會得到相同的分群結果。
4
探索洞察透過三個檢視來閱讀分析結果：人物誌看板、歷程地圖、數據分析與資料庫。

你會得到什麼

人物誌看板每一群各有一頁：人口統計特徵、一天的工作內容、常用工具、痛點、關鍵字與心聲。可以編輯內容。
歷程地圖呈現該群在各個階段的滿意度、行動、工具與痛點，並為每個階段提出優化機會。
數據分析與資料庫分群探索（雷達圖與熱度圖）、所有受訪者的分佈與填答圖表。

適用對象

產品負責人了解使用者與顧客的歷程，找出行為模式並發掘顧客在旅程中遇到的痛點。
UX 研究員建立有證據支持的使用者分群，用量化資料補強質性研究。
服務設計師看出不同族群的體驗差異，找到服務可以改善之處。
CX 顧問依行為特徵區分顧客，找出體驗落差。

資料隱私

quan-persona 採用本機優先的設計。問卷資料在你的裝置上、於瀏覽器內處理，不會上傳到雲端伺服器進行分析。你建立的自訂衡量架構，則儲存在該裝置瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。

這讓資料的掌控權留在你手上，也降低敏感問卷資料不必要的外洩風險。

你上傳的資料集也會保留在這台裝置的瀏覽器中。請勿在共用或公用電腦上上傳你的 CSV 檔案；若不得不使用，離開前請先使用「資料」選單中的「恢復預設數據」。
